IconTile adds `frame` (`surface` | `none`), `shape` (`squircle` | `square` | `circle`) and `size` (`fixed` | `fill`, clamped to `iconTileRecipe.fillMinSize`–`fillMaxSize`), so complete product launcher artwork, theme icon shapes and grid-sized icons use the shared tile. Defaults keep the framed 64 squircle.
