SegmentedControl pills accept a controlled `value={null}` that selects no pill, for filters that page their options (a decade of season years) while the chosen value sits on another page. Pressing a pill still reports its value. Connected and text presentations reject `null` with a TypeError. Web hides the sliding highlight when nothing is selected.
