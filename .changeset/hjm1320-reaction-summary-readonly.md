ChatMessage `reactions.summary.readOnly` (default `false`) draws the reaction counts for readers who cannot react (guests) in the same place beside the time as non-interactive caption text named by `label` (no pill surface, no press target), so products no longer render guest counts as their own caption in `actions`.
