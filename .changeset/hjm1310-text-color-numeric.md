Text gains a product `color` role that replaces the tone color while keeping HJM metrics and font face, and a `numeric` axis (`tabular`/`proportional`) for stable digits on Web and Native. Native also exports `useHjmTextFace` so product text that cannot be a Text keeps the Provider's font face instead of falling back to the OS font.
